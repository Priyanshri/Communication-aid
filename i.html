Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We've got answers. If you don't see your query here, feel free to reach out to our support team.

Who can benefit from using Communication Aid?

Our tools are designed for a wide range of users, including individuals with speech or hearing impairments, people in multilingual environments, public speakers, educators, and anyone looking to make communication more accessible and efficient.

Do I need to install any software?

No, Communication Aid is a fully web-based application. You can access all our features directly from your browser on any device with an internet connection. There’s no need to download or install anything.

How accurate is the speech-to-text transcription?

We use state-of-the-art AI models to provide highly accurate transcriptions in real-time. Accuracy can be affected by factors like background noise and speaker clarity, but our system is continuously learning and improving.

When will the Sign Language AI feature be available?

Our Sign Language AI is currently in active development. We are working hard to ensure it's accurate and reliable before its public release. Please sign up for our newsletter to receive updates on its launch!
